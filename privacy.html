Privacy Policy

Last updated September 25, 2026

Welcome Canada does not collect, store or share any personal information.

Your study progress, your settings and anything you type into the app (such as your test date or the names of your representatives) are saved on your device and, if you're signed in to iCloud, in your own iCloud account so they come back if you reinstall the app or get a new iPhone. We can't see that iCloud copy. The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it never connects to a server of its own. Study reminders, if you turn them on, are scheduled on your device.

The one optional purchase, Unlock Unlimited Access, is handled entirely by Apple's App Store: we never see or store your payment details.

Deleting the app deletes the data on your device. The iCloud copy stays in your iCloud account so your progress can come back if you reinstall; resetting your progress in the app first clears your study history from it too.
