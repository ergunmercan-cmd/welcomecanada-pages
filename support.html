Support

Welcome Canada is a study app for the Canadian citizenship test. It works offline and needs no account.

Common questions

How do the free 15 minutes work?
Every calendar day, the app gives you 15 minutes of full access at no cost, resetting at local midnight. Unlock Unlimited Access ($9.99, one payment) removes that daily limit for good.
I bought Unlock Unlimited Access on another iPhone. How do I get it back?
Open Settings inside the app and tap Restore Purchases while signed in to the same Apple Account you purchased with.
Does my progress carry over to a new iPhone?
Yes, if you're signed in to iCloud. Progress is copied to your own iCloud account and comes back automatically on a reinstall or a new device.
How do I turn off the arrival sound or reminders?
Open Settings inside the app to turn off arrival music or study reminders at any time.
How do I reset my progress?
Settings inside the app has a reset option. This clears your study history on that device (and in iCloud, once it syncs).
